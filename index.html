A superb opportunity to purchase a bright, well-proportioned one-bedroom apartment in the heart of Dublin City Centre. No. 2 Jervis Place is a first-floor residence extending to approximately 39 sq. m. The accommodation is well arranged, with an entrance hallway leading to a separate kitchen and a bright living/dining room. A generous double bedroom and main bathroom complete the layout, creating a comfortable and well-balanced living space. With the Luas Red Line practically on the doorstep providing excellent connections across Dublin and beyond. Shops, cafés, restaurants, bars, cultural attractions and entertainment are all within a short stroll. Combining city-centre convenience with excellent accessibility, Jervis Place is well suited to both owner-occupiers and investors, with strong rental potential in a highly accessible location.
